Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CareerLens and how it works

How does the AI resume analyser Work?

+
Our AI analyzes your resume against thousands of successful resumes and job descriptions. It checks for ATS compatibility, keyword optimization, formatting, and content quality to provide actionable feedback for improvement.

Is CareerLens free to use?

+
CareerLens offers a range of pricing plans, including a free tier with basic features and premium plans for advanced tools and resources. Visit our pricing page for more details.

How do I get started with CareerLens?

+
Getting started is easy! Simply sign up for an account, upload your resume, and let our AI analyze it. You'll receive personalized feedback and recommendations to improve your resume.

How accurate is the internship Matching?

+
Our AI matching algorithm has a 95% accuracy rate, analyzing your skills, interests, and career goals against our database of 10,000+ internships. We continuously improve our matching based on successful placements.

Can I track my application Progress?

+
Yes! Our career dashboard provides comprehensive tracking of all your applications, including status updates, interview schedules, and follow-up reminders. Never miss an opportunity again.

What file formats do you support for resumes?

+
We support PDF, DOC, DOCX, and TXT formats. PDF is recommended for best analysis results. Our AI can extract and analyze content from any of these formats effectively.

How quickly can I see results?

+
Resume analysis is instant! Internship matches appear within seconds of uploading your profile. Most students see significant improvement in their application success rate within the first week.
